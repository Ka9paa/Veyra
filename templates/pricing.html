Before you purchase

Is paid billing live in this beta?

The site is Stripe-payment-link ready. Do not accept real paid subscriptions until Stripe payment links plus subscription webhooks and credit updates are configured and tested.

Where are payment details stored?

Stripe Checkout handles the payment information. Veyra should store only the resulting customer/subscription identifiers needed for account access and billing state.

Can I start without paying?

Yes. New accounts begin with starter credits so people can test the builder before choosing a paid plan.
